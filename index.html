Chapter III

Poetry

Some feelings are easier to write than to say.

I don't think I could ever find the perfect words for everything you mean to me.

But maybe I can try.

I

If I could keep a moment,
I'd keep the ones with you.
The quiet little memories,
the ordinary things we do.

II

I'd keep the sound of your laughter,
the way you make me smile,
the little conversations
that make the world feel worthwhile.

III

I never needed fireworks
or something grand and new.
Somehow the smallest moments
became special because of you.

IV

And if tomorrow changes,
if the seasons come and go,
I hope I'll still remember
the happiness you helped me know.

V

Because somewhere in this story,
between every laugh and every day,
you became a little piece
of the happiness I want to stay.

VI

So if these words seem simple,
there's a reason why they do.

Sometimes the simplest words
are the truest ones.

✦

And if I had to write this story all over again...

I'd still choose the part where I met you.

♡

But there are still things I want to tell you.
