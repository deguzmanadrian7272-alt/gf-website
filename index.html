But I think this was where our story really began.

Chapter III

Poetry

Some feelings are easier to write than they are to say.

For You
✦

Maybe it was your smile

that made an ordinary day feel a little less ordinary.

Maybe it was your voice,

the little conversations, the laughter, and the moments I wished could last longer.

Maybe it was never one particular thing.

Maybe it was simply you being you.

And somewhere between all those little moments,

I found myself caring more than I ever expected to.

If I could put into words everything you mean to me,

I think I'd still need a thousand poems to say it all.

So for now, I'll simply say...

I'm really glad I met you.
