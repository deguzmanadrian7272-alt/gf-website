Dear Princess,

If I had to explain what makes you special to me, I'm not sure where I would begin. Dahil sa totoo lang, habang sinusulat ko ang mga mensahing ito. Hindi ko alam kung ano pa nga ba ang p'wede kong sabihin. Dahil kahit ano pang sabihin ko, Lahat na yata ng bagay na nakita't na ranasan ko mula sa'yo'y dahilan nito. Walang nag bibigay rason sa'kin para makitang mababa, Tama, O sakto lng ang halaga mo. Dahil para sa'kin sobra ka pa sa dapat na na tatanggap ko.

Perhaps I would start with all the little things I've noticed about you. The way you make ordinary moments feel different. The way even a simple conversation with you can become something I find myself remembering later. The way you see the world. The way you think and act diferrently from the others. I did not expect that having you in my life would be my biggest plot twist this year. You've become the surprise I never saw coming, and yet, somehow, it feels like everything has fallen into place in ways I didn't know I would wish not to change because that's what I've always wanted.

There is something about you that makes the world feel a little softer, a little warmer, and somehow a little more beautiful. And even I can't call it "little", Because it is a lot to me more than what I can say, More than what you can see, And more than what you think. I found everything about you as cool and amazing all the time. And please do remember, There is a quiet kind of love that asks for nothing in return. I know where I stand in your life, and because I care about you, I'll never force my feelings into a place where they don't belong. Kahit mahal kita, Pipiliin kong igalang ang mga hangganang itinakda mo. Hindi kita pipiliting suklian ang nararamdaman ko. I'll simply remain here supporting you from a distance, Celebrating your victories, And praying for your happiness, Even if I'm not the reason behind your smile. Because that is how much i worth you.

And perhaps that's what I like most — you don't have to try to make things special.

Somehow, just being you already does that. being you worth than what you think. Being the person you really are, makes it more than what i wish and would want

Yours, always. My Love

One Last Thing... III

Dear Love,

You do not owe me anything. I did everything out of love. So whatever I gave to you, is yours to keep. Because I truly believe that love isn't meant to be transactional. I know that there are some depths in love. And there are people who are scared to give love and receive love. And I honestly one of them. And me showing it as my weakness and tampo, isn't because nag kulang ka. but because just like the others. i do have weakness and wants, that aren't always going to be meet. But know that what I gave, Is what I want, Not out of obligations, Or out of reciprocity.

Just because I tell you I love you a thousand times, Doesn't mean I mean it any less. Sometimes I say it because I still can't believe how lucky I am to have you in my life. Having you is more than enough.. Much more than enough actually. I didn't fall in love with you because of what you can do for me, Or for your body or looks, I fell in love with you because you brought back my smile, you made me understand what love really feels like, you made me feel safe and cared for, you were my peace. And I am forever grateful for that.

There's nothing more liberating and freeing than the thought of having to give love because I want to, And not just because I want something from you. This is what I'm gonna do, and going to do, Because this is what I want to do for you. Because I care for you, And I love you as a person and who you are. May we both find ourselves in a situation wherein there is always a healthy balance between us and everything in between—And have calm over chaos in a world full of uncertainty.

When the world overwhelmes you, I'll be there to listen to your fears. I'll hold your hand in your darkness night and cherish our brightest moments. I want to be good for you, I want to be the love you need.

Kaya't nung kinaya kong timbangin kung anong mas malalim sa puso kong nagmamahal at sa bulsa kong walang laman—nakita kita at wala akong kailangan timbangin dahil nagsukli ka pa. Hindi magiging lason ang pagmamahal kapag ako ang taong kasama mo.

And I want our relation to have God within.

We've talked about the beginning of our story. We've talked about the little things, the moments, and the feelings that slowly grew along the way. And now, I think there's one more thing I want you to know. Every chapter I've written so far has been leading to something. Not because I knew exactly how our story would end.. But because I know there are still pages I'd like to write with you. Maybe the next chapter shouldn't just be something I write for you. Maybe it should be something we write together.

With all my heart,
Me. Your memeboy 😒

I

The Things I Hope Never Change

The future is unpredictable, and it has a way of changing everything around us as time goes on. But through all the shifts and uncertainties, there are some things I hope never fade. I hope the connection we share, the quiet comfort we find in each other, and the small everyday moments stay exactly the same.

Forget the big promises and the picture-perfect plans. What matters most to me are the little things that belong only to us—the random habits, inside jokes, and daily routines that make our story feel special and completely different from anyone else’s.

  • I hope we never stop sharing our day with each other.
  • I hope random conversations never become boring.
  • I hope we keep finding reasons to stay up too late.
  • I hope we never stop making ordinary days special.
  • I hope we'll always choose to stay with each other
  • I hope the time will feels like so short whenever we're talking
  • I hope we never make a big deal out of awkward moments.
  • I hope misunderstandings never turn into arguments.
  • I hope we never let pride get in the way of what's in our hearts.
  • I hope these little things stay with us, always.
II

Little Things ♡

There is a quiet magic in the ordinary, and that’s what I want most with you. Forget the big occasions—I just want the small, unscheduled pieces of our day. The shared looks, the random laughs, and the simple comfort of knowing we're just existing in the same space together.

Distance has a way of changing how we see things. The moments that seem small and fleeting when viewed from afar are often the very ones that linger the longest. It's the quiet stillness, the casual glance, and the unnoticed warmth between us that silently build the foundation of everything we'll remember.
