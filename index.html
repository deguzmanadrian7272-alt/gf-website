Chapter II

The Little Things

Sometimes, it's not the big moments that make YOU special. It's all the little things.

01

The Little Things I Notice

I find myself falling for all the little details: your random yapping, our out-of-the-blue voice calls, and every photo and video you share with me. I love that there's something special in the way you treat me that no one else gets. Seeing how beautifully you treat the people you care about makes me wish, maybe a little greedily, that I get the biggest piece of that love.

You think so differently from everyone around you, and I promise you, that unique spirit of yours is what I want to protect the most. I'll do whatever it takes to never lose your trust.I just love how you see the world, your eagerness to keep learning, and the quiet, attentive way you take the time to truly know and understand me.

02

The Little Things You Do

I adore the little things you do for me: the "how are you" and "how was your day?" texts, that became our routine each night. the way you always check on me, and how you genuinely want to hear me yap about anything and everything.

I love that we constantly learn from one another, and how one of us rambling always leads to exchanging deep thoughts and realizing just how much we share the same world.

03

The Random Moments

I find myself replaying our random moments—from that very first invite you gave me to play, to every single game we’ve shared since. Hearing your laughter mid-game has always been the best part; it turns the simplest match into pure joy.

And then there was that first movie we watched together. My mind was racing with quiet worry, second-guessing every scene and constantly wanting to ask if you were actually enjoying it or if you secretly disliked it. Yet, despite the nervousness, it remains the one moment I would give anything to live through all over again.

A Letter For You I

Dear Princess,

If I had to explain what makes you special to me, I'm not sure where I would begin.

Maybe I would start with all the little things I've noticed about you. The way you make ordinary moments feel different. The way even a simple conversation with you can become something I find myself remembering later.

There is something about you that makes the world feel a little softer, a little warmer, and somehow a little more beautiful.

And perhaps that's what I like most — you don't have to try to make things special.

Somehow, just being you already does that.

Yours, always.

The Things I Couldn't Say II

Dear You,

There are moments when I want to tell you how much you mean to me, but somehow the words never seem big enough.

I could tell you that I enjoy talking to you. I could tell you that I look forward to hearing from you.

But even those words don't quite explain it.

Because somewhere between the conversations, the laughter, the random moments, and everything in between, you became someone I genuinely care about.

Maybe I haven't always said it clearly enough.

So let this letter say what I sometimes struggle to put into words.

You matter to me.

Chapter VII

The Future

So... where do we go from here?

I

The Things I Hope Never Change

The future is unpredictable, and it has a way of changing everything around us as time goes on. But through all the shifts and uncertainties, there are some things I hope never fade. I hope the connection we share, the quiet comfort we find in each other, and the small everyday moments stay exactly the same.

Forget the big promises and the picture-perfect plans. What matters most to me are the little things that belong only to us—the random habits, inside jokes, and daily routines that make our story feel special and completely different from anyone else’s.

  • I hope we never stop sharing our day with each other.
  • I hope random conversations never become boring.
  • I hope we keep finding reasons to stay up too late.
  • I hope we never stop making ordinary days special.
  • I hope we'll always choose to stay with each other
  • I hope the time will feels like so short whenever we're talking
  • I hope we never make a big deal out of awkward moments.
  • I hope misunderstandings never turn into arguments.
  • I hope we never let pride get in the way of what's in our hearts.
  • I hope these little things stay with us, always.
II

Little Things ♡

There is a quiet magic in the ordinary, and that’s what I want most with you. Forget the big occasions—I just want the small, unscheduled pieces of our day. The shared looks, the random laughs, and the simple comfort of knowing we're just existing in the same space together.

Distance has a way of changing how we see things. The moments that seem small and fleeting when viewed from afar are often the very ones that linger the longest. It's the quiet stillness, the casual glance, and the unnoticed warmth between us that silently build the foundation of everything we'll remember.
