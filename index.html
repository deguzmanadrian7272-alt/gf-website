The Day We Met

It all started on the day I first met you.

Chapter I · Page Two

My First Impression of You

I still remember the first things I noticed about you.

Maybe it was your smile. Maybe it was the way you talked. Maybe it was something so small that I didn't even realize how much it would stay with me.

Whatever it was, there was something about you that caught my attention.

Chapter I · Page Three

Then We Started Talking

Little by little, our conversations became something I started looking forward to.

We talked about random things. We laughed about things that probably wouldn't make sense to anyone else.

And somehow, without either of us realizing it, those little conversations started becoming some of my favorite moments.

Chapter I · Page Four

Somewhere Along the Way...

I don't know exactly when it happened.

There wasn't a specific day when everything suddenly changed.

It was probably a collection of little moments — the conversations, the laughs, the random messages, and the times I found myself thinking about you when you weren't even around.

Somewhere along the way, you became someone important to me.

✿

Chapter I · Page Five

And Then I Realized...

Looking back now, I think I slowly started realizing just how special you had become to me.

You weren't just another person I happened to meet.

You became someone whose presence could make an ordinary day feel a little better.

And maybe I didn't have the words for it back then...

But I think this was where our story really began.

A Letter For You I

Dear Princess,

If I had to explain what makes you special to me, I'm not sure where I would begin.

Maybe I would start with all the little things I've noticed about you. The way you make ordinary moments feel different. The way even a simple conversation with you can become something I find myself remembering later.

There is something about you that makes the world feel a little softer, a little warmer, and somehow a little more beautiful.

And perhaps that's what I like most — you don't have to try to make things special.

Somehow, just being you already does that.

Yours, always.

♡
❦
The Things I Couldn't Say II

Dear You,

There are moments when I want to tell you how much you mean to me, but somehow the words never seem big enough.

I could tell you that I enjoy talking to you. I could tell you that I look forward to hearing from you.

But even those words don't quite explain it.

Because somewhere between the conversations, the laughter, the random moments, and everything in between, you became someone I genuinely care about.

Maybe I haven't always said it clearly enough.

So let this letter say what I sometimes struggle to put into words.

You matter to me.

♡

One Last Thing... III

Dear Princess,

We've talked about the beginning of our story.

We've talked about the little things, the moments, and the feelings that slowly grew along the way.

And now, I think there's one more thing I want you to know.

Every chapter I've written so far has been leading to something.

Not because I knew exactly how our story would end...

But because I know there are still pages I'd like to write with you.

Maybe the next chapter shouldn't just be something I write for you.

Maybe it should be something we write together.

With all my heart,
Me.
